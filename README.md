# Coffee-Shop-Sales-Analysis
☕ **Coffee Shop Sales**: Interactive Data Analysis & Dashboard

📌 **Project Overview**

This project focuses on analyzing transaction data for a coffee shop chain to identify key drivers of sales, peak operational hours, and product performance. I transformed raw transactional data into an interactive executive dashboard that allows stakeholders to make data-driven decisions regarding staffing and inventory.

🚀 **The Business Problem**

The shop management needed to answer three critical questions:

1. When is the shop busiest? (Staffing optimization)

2. Which products drive the most revenue? (Inventory & Marketing)

3. How does performance vary across different locations? (Regional Analysis)

🛠️ **Tech Stack & Tools**

Excel: Data cleaning, Pivot Tables, and Dashboard design.

Power Query: Data transformation (Time-to-seconds conversion for duration analysis) and quality checks.

Power Query: To handle and shape the initial dataset.

🧹 **Data Cleaning & Transformation**

To ensure the data was "analysis-ready," I performed the following steps:

Standardized Sizes: Identified and re-labeled product sizes that were not defined to ensure accurate category reporting.

Time Correction: Fixed legacy formatting issues where transaction times were incorrectly showing default date values (1899-12-31).

Feature Engineering: Created new columns for Hour of Day and Day of the Week to allow for deep-dive seasonal analysis.

📊 **Key Insights**

1. Peak Hours: Sales peak consistently between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, accounting for nearly 30% of daily volume.

2. Product Mix: While Coffee is the top revenue generator (45%), Tea shows a surprising 15% growth trend on Monday mornings.

3. Average Transaction Value (ATV): The average customer spends $4.65 per visit, with "Large" sized drinks being the most frequent choice.

📈 **Dashboard Features**

**Interactive Slicers:** Users can filter the entire dashboard by Day of the week, Location, and Month categories.

**Dynamic KPI Cards:** Real-time updates for Total Sales, Total Orders, Ave Bill per Person, and Ave Order per Person.

**Visual Hierarchy:** Designed with a "Top-Down" flow, moving from high-level totals to specific product performance.

📂 **How to View**

Download the Coffee Shop Sales.xlsx file.

Please open in Excel and click "Enable Content" for the slicers to function.

Use the filters on the top/side to explore the data!
